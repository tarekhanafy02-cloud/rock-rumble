Rock Rumble

You’re the gold rock. The camera follows you. Point or tap where you want to roll, dash into rivals, and knock them into the lava. Last rock standing wins. Every map is different. Grab the glowing gems for Mega Rock or Quake. Earn 5 points per KO and 10 per win, then spend them in the Shop.

Keys: arrows or WASD to move, space to dash. Q and E turn the camera.
